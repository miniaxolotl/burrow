Burrow
======

Burrow gives a local app a public HTTPS link. Share a preview, receive webhooks and inspect requests in your terminal. Available for macOS and Linux. Open source and self-hostable. Install with npm i -g @miniaxolotl/burrowctl.

Website: https://useburrow.app/
